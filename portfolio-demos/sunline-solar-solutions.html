Frequently Asked Questions

Are solar installations compliant with DEWA regulations?
Yes, all Sunline Solar Solutions systems strictly follow DEWA’s Shams Dubai regulations and technical guidelines.
How do panels handle Dubai's extreme summer heat and sand?
We utilize high-temperature-rated Tier-1 panels engineered with specialized anti-dust coatings and offer scheduled cleaning services.

Our solar panels come with a 25-year linear performance warranty and 10-year product warranty.
