FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Sunline Solar systems fully DEWA compliant?
Yes, all installations strictly adhere to DEWA’s Shams Dubai regulations, electrical standards, and structural grid connection approvals.
How do panels handle high summer temperatures in Dubai?
We utilize specialized high-temperature coefficient Tier-1 panels engineered to prevent thermal degradation during extreme summer heat spikes.
What is the expected system lifespan and warranty?
Our solar panels carry a 25-year linear performance warranty and a 10 to 12-year manufacturer product warranty.
